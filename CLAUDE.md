### Overview

This is a web-based rpg-style game that simulates day to day tasks of a fictitional car dealership. Overall goal of the game is to establish a rich client base, and increase revenue.

### Architecture

**Decided stack (Phase 1):** Vite + TypeScript + React Three Fiber (`@react-three/fiber`, `@react-three/drei`) + zustand. Isometric orthographic camera, low-poly style.

### Conventions

- `src/sim/` is pure TypeScript (no React or three.js imports) and unit tested with Vitest. Grid, pathfinding, layout and interactable definitions live here.
- `src/scene/` holds R3F components, `src/ui/` holds DOM HUD, `src/state/` holds the zustand store, `src/input/` holds input hooks.
- Per-frame values (player position, camera easing) live in refs or `useFrame`. Never call `setState` every frame. The store only holds discrete events.
- Phase plan: see `docs/SPEC.md` and the detailed `docs/PHASE1_PLAN.md`. Phase 1 is split into 1a (scaffold), 1b (movement and camera), 1c (environment), 1d (interactables and HUD).
- Phase 2 plan: `docs/PHASE2_PLAN.md`, split into 2a (character models), 2b (clock, money, inventory), 2c (customer sim), 2d (customers in the world), 2e (selling and day loop). Character GLBs are skinned: clone with `SkeletonUtils.clone`, drive clips through an `anim` ref (`scene/Character.tsx`).
- Phase 3 plan: `docs/PHASE3_PLAN.md`, split into 3a (staff foundation, hiring, receptionist), 3b (light avoidance), 3c (handlers and finance manager), 3d (AI salesperson), 3e (lot porter and cleanliness), 3f (pedestrians, archetypes, owner). Customers and staff share the walker core in `scene/walker.ts`; staff positions live in `runtime.staffPos`. Walkers avoid each other through `sim/crowd.ts` (goal-tile reservations, separation push, stuck guard); the player is never pushed. Deals are per handler: `Customer.handlerId` is `PLAYER_ID` or an employee id, so use `dealCustomer(customers, handlerId)` and never assume a single deal. Buyers handed off to finance wait in the `queued` phase until `callNextBuyer` frees the guest chair for them. A salesperson `claim`s a customer (sets `handlerId`, phase unchanged) before walking over; `sellerId` keeps who made the sale through a hand-off, and `chairId` is the guest chair a buyer walks to and signs in (office or a sales desk). Salesperson decisions are pure in `sim/staffAi.ts` (`nextSalesTask`); `scene/Staff.tsx` carries them out through the store's `staff*` actions. Car dirt lives on `InventoryCar.cleanliness` (`sim/cleanliness.ts`): dust overnight in `beginDay`, a little per browse visit in `commit`, washed back to 1 by the porter (`nextPorterTask`, `staffWash`) or the player's **Wash car**; it feeds `acceptChance` (±8%) and the car's paint tint in `scene/Props.tsx`. Customers have an `archetype` (`sim/archetypes.ts`) that shapes their traits in `generateCustomer` and adds to `acceptChance`; a couple's `companion` is a look only, walked by `scene/Customers`. Passers-by (`sim/pedestrians.ts`, `scene/Pedestrians.tsx`) and the owner's walk (`scene/Owner.tsx`) live outside the store: positions go in `runtime.ambientPos`, a walk-in becomes a customer through the store's `walkIn`, and the owner's goal (`sim/owner.ts`) is `owner` in the store, judged in `settleDay`.
- Phase 4 plan: `docs/PHASE4_PLAN.md`, split into 4a (touch input foundation), 4b (mobile HUD and on-screen controls), 4c (mobile performance and device QA). Mouse acts on `pointerdown` as before; touch and pen act on `pointerup` only when `isTap` (`input/touch.ts`, pure rules in `input/gestures.ts`), so a pinch never moves the player or opens a menu, and touch never sets the hover outline. Anything that swallows clicks must swallow `onPointerUp` too. Camera yaw changes go through `rotateView(dir)` in `scene/runtime.ts`; on touch a two-finger twist (`onTwist`) calls it in place of the ⟲ ⟳ buttons, which hide on `COARSE`. Layout breakpoints are `COMPACT`/`COARSE` in `input/useMediaQuery.ts` (mirrored in `ui/hud.css`); hints that name a key show the touch wording on `COARSE`, and every keyed action also has an on-screen button or gesture (`ui/ViewControls.tsx`, the status ✕, the Staff button). Position HUD panels with the `--edge-*` variables so they clear the notch. `rotatePrompt` (portrait on touch) pauses the game through `isPaused`.
- Phase 5 plan: `docs/PHASE5_PLAN.md`, split into 5a (dealer cost and profit), 5b (ordering, delivery and floor plan), 5c (stock panel, office computer, help). Each `InventoryCar` has a dealer `cost`; a `Sale` records `msrp` and `cost`, so the summary shows gross profit (`grossProfit` in `sim/deal.ts`) and `netIncome` is gross less wages, commissions and floor plan interest. Stock is ordered through `sim/ordering.ts` (`placeOrder` into the first of `freeSlots`, showroom platforms first) and held in the store's `orders` until `beginDay` delivers it, after dropping sold cars. A car is paid in cash or `floored` (`sim/floorPlan.ts`): the bank is repaid its `cost` out of the sale price in `sellCar`, or early with `payOff`, and `settleDay` charges `dailyInterest`. `DayStats.missed` counts customers who found none of their body types in stock. Salespeople earn `salesCommission(price, cost)` (`sim/staff.ts`): a share of the gross with a `MIN_COMMISSION` floor. Stock is ordered in `ui/StockPanel.tsx` (`stockOpen`, opened from the office computer's `orderStock` action, the top-bar **Office** button or `I`).
- Phase 6 plan: `docs/PHASE6_PLAN.md` (negotiation), split into 6b (player negotiation) and 6c (staff haggle by skill); 6a was folded into 5a. Prices are haggled in `sim/negotiation.ts`: `respondToAsk` answers an ask with accept, counter or walk, from the customer's `expect` (archetype `haggle.expect` ± `EXPECT_JITTER`) and `haggle.rounds`. A counter sends them back to `talking` with `Customer.haggle` (`round`, `lastAsk`, `counter`; null until the first counter, cleared by `cancel`), and `offer` is null until the next ask. The player asks through the store's `ask(price)` (clamped by `askRange`), the menu's offer uses `suggestedAsk`, and the haggle controls live in `ui/CustomerPanel.tsx`. Salespeople haggle through `staffAsk(skill, car, haggle)` (open at MSRP from skill 3, concede `staffConcession(skill)` of the gap, take a counter when green or when it keeps a healthy gross, never under cost + `STAFF_FLOOR_MARGIN`); `scene/Staff.tsx` restarts the pitch timer on each answer and re-pitches after `SALES_COUNTER_SECONDS`. The summary's per-seller table shows `averageDiscount` off MSRP.
- Phase 7 plan: `docs/PHASE7_PLAN.md` (marketing and improvements), split into 7a (marketing campaigns), 7b (outdoor improvements), 7c (indoor improvements), 7d (reputation and referrals). Ads are timed `Campaign`s in `sim/marketing.ts` (`CHANNELS`, paid up front by the store's `launchCampaign`, starting the next morning); `beginDay` drops finished ones and plans arrivals with `trafficBoost` (repeat runs of a channel fall off by `REPEAT_FALLOFF`). Each arrival has a `Source` (`ArrivalSchedule.sources`, `Customer.source`, `Sale.source`) whose `sourceWeights` skew `pickArchetype`. `DayStats.marketing` is the day's ad spend (taken off `netIncome`), `bySource` counts visitors, and the summary's source table comes from `salesBySource`. The office computer panel has Stock and Marketing tabs (`computerTab`; I and M, the top-bar **Office** button, or the computer's `orderStock`/`advertise`). Improvements (7b) are a catalogue in `sim/improvements.ts` (`IMPROVEMENTS`, tiers per `slot`, only the top tier counts in `effectsOf`), bought through the store's `buyImprovement` and kept as `improvements: { id, day }[]`; they go up overnight (`installed(owned, day)`). The scene reads what's up: `scene/Pedestrians` plans passers-by with `effectsOf` once a day, `scene/runtime` blocks `improvementFootprints` each morning, the sign in `scene/Props.tsx` draws its tier and `scene/TubeMan.tsx` flails in `useFrame`. Upgrades tab: `computerTab` `'upgrades'`, key U or the computer's `improve`. Indoor improvements (7c) are in the same catalogue with an `area`: they add props (`improvementProps`, blocking via `improvementFootprints`) or swap a fixed prop's model (`swappedModel`), and `scene/useUpNow` gives components what's up. Their effects are applied in the store: `expectCut` scales a new customer's `expect` in `generateCustomer`, `acceptBonus` adds to `sellerBonus`, `patienceSaved` slows the drain in `tickClock`. Reputation (7d) is the store's `reputation` (0–100, `sim/reputation.ts`), moved only in `settleDay` by `reputationChange(dayStats)` (sales up; refused, impatient and missed down; capped per day) and recorded as `DayStats.reputation`; `beginDay` passes it to `planArrivals` (`visitorScale`, `referralVisitors` with `source: 'referral'`) and to `trafficBoost` (`campaignScale`). The owner's `reputation` goal is judged on the live `reputationChange`. Save v7 keeps it.
- Phase 8 plan: `docs/PHASE8_PLAN.md` (audio), split into 8a (audio foundation and settings), 8b (sound effects), 8c (music), 8d (gibberish voices). `src/audio/` is the Web Audio side (no React or three.js): it reacts to the store through `subscribe` and is never called from `sim/`. The decisions (cue, track, voice, who talks next) are pure in `sim/`. Audio settings (`sim/audioSettings.ts`: `effectiveGain` folds master, mute and the `PAUSE_DUCK` of sfx/voice into each bus) are the store's `audio`, changed by `setVolume`/`toggleMute`, and kept in localStorage by `startAudioPref`, not in the save. `audio/engine.ts` makes no AudioContext until the first gesture, then `audioContext()`/`busNode(bus)` are what sounds play through; `audio/samples.ts` (`playSfx`) caches decoded files from `public/audio/` (credited in its `LICENSE.md`). The sound panel (`ui/AudioPanel.tsx`, `audioOpen`) opens from the top bar's speaker or the title screen; N mutes. Sound effects (8b) are decided by `sfxFor(prev, next)` in `sim/sfxEvents.ts`, a diff of two store states into `SfxCue`s with an optional subject (customer, employee or car); `audio/sfxBridge.ts` plays them through `allowCue` (a minimum gap per cue) and `spatialMix` (distance from `playerPos`, pan by screen x from `cameraState.yaw`). HUD clicks go through `playUiCue`. A new cue needs a `SfxCue`, a file (or synth) in `audio/samples.ts`, a level in the bridge's `VOLUME` and a credit in `public/audio/LICENSE.md`. Music (8c): `trackFor` in `sim/musicPlan.ts` picks the `TrackId` (title, the day parts by `NOON_MINUTE`/`RUSH_MINUTE`, or summary once `dayOver`), and `isMuffled` (guide or rotate prompt) adds a low-pass. `audio/music.ts` streams `public/audio/music/<track>.ogg|mp3` through one `HTMLAudioElement` per track, crossfades on store changes only, and starts on unlock through `syncMusic`. Voices (8d): `sim/gibberish.ts` gives each character a seeded `voiceOf(id, variant)` and turns a `Tone` into syllables (`utterance`); `sim/chatter.ts` decides who talks: `conversationsOf` + `nextLine` for turn-taking (pitch, signing, couple), `reactionsFor(prev, next)` for one-off lines, `voiceMix` for distance (skipped past `VOICE_FAR`). `scene/Chatter.tsx` keeps the timers in module state, plays at most `MAX_VOICES` nearest lines through `audio/voice.ts` (`speak`), and freezes while `isPaused`.
- Phase 9 plan: `docs/PHASE9_PLAN.md` (adversaries), split into 9a (Nazma's visits and smudged cars), 9b (security guard), 9c (overnight theft), 9d (poaching and counter-offers). Nazma is he/him. Nazma's visits (9a) are decided in `sim/nazma.ts`: `isNazmaDay(day, guarded)` (always on `FIRST_NAZMA_DAY`, then a seeded roll) and `planVisit` (2–3 cars, lot first, an `arrivalMinute`), kept as the store's `nazma` (`status` coming → onLot → done or runOff, `progress` through `targets`) and rebuilt each morning in `beginDay`, never saved. `scene/Nazma.tsx` walks him (position in `runtime.ambientPos` only) and reports through `nazmaArrived`, `nazmaSmudge` (`smudgeCar` in `sim/cleanliness.ts`), `nazmaRunOff(by)` and `nazmaLeft`. The player's `confront` action targets `NAZMA_ID` (`findInteractable`, blocked by `confrontBlocker`); `scene/Player` re-plans the chase as he moves and confronts within `CONFRONT_REACH`. The day's tally is `DayStats.nazma` (`nazmaSummary` for the summary line). The security guard (9b) is the `security` role: `isGuarded(roster)` feeds `isNazmaDay` in `beginDay`, and `nextGuardTask` in `sim/staffAi.ts` walks `GUARD_PATROL_TILES` or chases Nazma once he's within `guardSight(skill)`; `scene/Staff.tsx` reads him from `ambientPos` and calls `nazmaRunOff('guard')` on contact. The save accepts only known roles. Overnight theft (9c): `isTheftNight(day)` replays earlier nights for the `THEFT_GAP_DAYS` gap (nothing saved), and `planTheft` takes a lot car weighted by MSRP, or is `foiled` when `isGuarded`. `beginDay` applies it after `dropSold` (cash pays the `cost` of a floored car), records `DayStats.nazma.stolen`/`foiled`, and `theftLoss` comes off `netIncome`. Loading a save replays the same morning, so a theft never repeats. Poaching (9d): `planVisit(rng, inventory, roster)` sometimes (`POACH_CHANCE`) picks scheme `poach`, aimed at one `isPoachable` employee (skill-weighted) in `targets`. `scene/Nazma` follows them, calls `nazmaChat` on reaching them (`NazmaVisit.chatting` drives the `poach` conversation in `sim/chatter.ts`) and `nazmaPoach` after `POACH_SECONDS`, which sets `Employee.quitting` through `reduceStaff`'s `poached` event. `keepEmployee` (`keep` event, `retentionRaise`) clears it with a raise; at `close` a quitter becomes `fired` but keeps `quitting`, so `payroll` still pays the day. `DayStats.nazma` records `poached`, `quit` and `kept`. Save v8 always writes `quitting: false`.
- Phase 10 plan: `docs/PHASE10_PLAN.md` (calendar and events), split into 10a (calendar and weekly rhythm), 10b (weather), 10c (manufacturer quota), 10d (sales events and closeouts). The calendar (`sim/calendar.ts`) is derived from the day number, never stored: `calendarOf(day)` (day 1 is Monday of week 1, January; 7-day weeks, 28-day months), `formatDate` for the top bar, `longDate` for the summary and title screen. `weekdayTraffic(day)` (`WEEKDAY_TRAFFIC`) scales `Word.scale` in `beginDay` (and day 1's initial plan) and the passers-by count through `planPedestrians`' `traffic`. The office computer's **Calendar** tab (`computerTab` `'calendar'`, key C, or the computer's `calendar` action) is `ui/CalendarTab.tsx`. Weather (10b) is `weatherOn(day)` in `sim/weather.ts` (month odds plus carry-over, replayed from day 1 and memoised; `forecastFor` for the Calendar tab), kept as the store's `weather` from `beginDay`, never saved. `WEATHER_EFFECTS` scales `Word.scale` and the passers-by (`planPedestrians`' `traffic` and `walkInScale`), `dirtyOvernight`'s `lotDirt`, and the patience drain of customers `waitingOutside` (the `tick` event's `outside`/`outsideFactor`). The scene reads it through `scene/useWeather` (lights and rain fog in `scene/Weather.tsx`, wet floors via `wetLook`); `audio/rain.ts` plays synthesized rain on the music bus when `rainPlays`. The manufacturer quota (10c) is `sim/quota.ts`: the store's `monthSales` (added to in `sign`) and `quota` (set in `beginDay` on the 1st by `monthlyQuota`, which resets `monthSales` too) are saved (v9). `settleDay` on the month's last day pays `holdback` (nothing under `HOLDBACK_FLOOR`, more at and past the target) into cash and records `DayStats.quota`, which `netIncome` adds. The top bar's `QuotaMeter` and the Calendar tab use `quotaStatus`/`quotaLine`. Sales events (10d) are `sim/events.ts`, derived from the day like the calendar (nothing saved): `eventOn(day)` (Fri–Sun of each `EVENTS` week) multiplies `Word.scale` in `beginDay` by its `traffic`; the store's `arrivalOpts` passes every new customer the event's `extraDiscount` (added to `expect`) and `skew` (archetype odds via `skewWeights`) along with the improvements' `expectCut`. `eventNotice` warns a week ahead and on opening day, `generateGoal`'s `onSale` asks for more sales, and `sfxFor` plays `fanfare` (synthesized) instead of the bell on a sale morning. `closeoutOn(day)` takes `CLOSEOUT_REBATE` off one model's invoice in `orderCost` for the month's last 3 days.

Money (11a): `startingCash`, `orderCost`/`placeOrder`'s `invoice` factor and `dailyInterest`'s `rate`. - Phase 12 plan: `docs/PHASE12_PLAN.md` (trade-ins and used cars), split into 12a (used stock foundation), 12b (driving in and customer parking), 12c (sellers), 12d (trade-ins in a sale), 12e (used-car buyers and aging). Used stock (12a) is `sim/usedCars.ts`: `InventoryCar.used` is a `UsedInfo` (year, miles, condition, `acquiredDay`) or null for a new car; `marketValue(model, info, day)` ages the new price by years, miles and condition, then loses `DAILY_DEPRECIATION` per day held; `appraise` is off by `appraisalNoise(skill)` (the player is `PLAYER_SKILL`); a used car's `msrp` is `usedListPrice` and it's built by `usedStockCar` (cash, id `used-<day>-<n>`). Driving in (12b) is `sim/driving.ts`: `CUSTOMER_PARKING` (3 spaces in `layout.ts`, never stock slots) and `Customer.vehicle` (`car`, `spot`, `parked`), given by `assignVehicles` to `DRIVE_IN_CHANCE` of the planned arrivals in `tickClock` while `freeSpot` has room (its own `driveRng`, so the rest of the day plays as before). `scene/DrivenCar.tsx` drives each car along the fixed `inboundRoute`/`outboundRoute` in `useFrame` (position in `runtime.vehiclePos`, never the store), stops for anyone `blockedAhead` (pushing on after a while), and reports `parked` (the driver's `arrive`); a leaving driver walks back to `doorTile` and scene/Customers dispatches `droveOff` instead of `despawn`. Sellers (12c) are `sim/sellers.ts`: `assignSellers` makes `SELLER_CHANCE` of drivers sellers (`Customer.selling`: `hope`, the player's `estimate`, `appraised`; no browse cars, they wait by their car, `makeOffer` instead of `greet`, and staff skip them). Trade-ins (12d) are `sim/tradeIns.ts`: `Customer.trade` (`hope`, `estimate`, `appraised`) for `TRADE_CHANCE` of shopping drivers (`assignTrades`). `Offer.allowance` is set only when the trade is in the deal (`tradeAllowance` in the store: a trade and a free lot space); with one, `respondToAsk`/`dealWarmth`/`askRange`/`suggestedAsk` take the allowance and the haggle's `lastAsk`/`counter` are nets (price − allowance), `Haggle.allowance` the last allowance. `sign` buys the trade as a `Purchase` at its allowance (`boughtRecord(…, true)`), records `Sale.trade` and dispatches `signed` with `traded`. Used-car buyers and aging (12e): archetype `used-shopper` (small budget, more body types; a fifth as many while no used car is for sale, through `usedStockSkew` in `arrivalOpts`, and those counted in `DayStats.missedUsed`) and each archetype's `usedWeight` on browsing used cars; a used car is judged by `fairPrice(car, day)` (today's `marketValue` as a sticker, falling while its `msrp` stays put), so `hopePrice`, `acceptChance` (`valueHeadroom`, `conditionBonus`), `respondToAsk` and `dealWarmth` take a trailing `day`. `Sale.used` feeds `grossSplit` in the summary; `isStale` (`STALE_DAYS`) flags stock; the `classifieds` channel's `driveIn`/`sellers` raise drive-in and seller odds (`driveInChance`, `sellerChance`); tip `staleUsed`.
